New to wallets? Start here
  1. Open this site in Chrome, Edge or Firefox with MetaMask installed from its official site. The in-app browser may not support wallet extensions.
  2. Create a wallet yourself. Keep your recovery phrase private; this app never asks for it.
  3. Connect below and approve switching to Polygon Amoy, chain ID 80002.
  4. Get free test POL from the Polygon faucet or Chainlink faucet. No purchase is needed.
  5. Deploy the registry once. Create a second wallet account for the recycler; give both accounts free test POL.
  6. Register a local batch. Publish its collection as the collector, then record receipt and switch to the nominated recycler account to publish the receipt.
